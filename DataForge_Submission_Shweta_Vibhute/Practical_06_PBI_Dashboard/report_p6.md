## 1. Problem Statement

The objective of this practical was to design a one-page Power BI executive dashboard that presents key business insights. The dashboard includes KPI cards, interactive charts, ranking visuals, and slicers to analyze sales performance by time, category, region, and products.

---

## 2. Concept Elaborated

Power BI is a business intelligence tool used to transform raw data into meaningful insights through interactive dashboards and reports. KPI cards summarize important metrics, charts help identify trends and comparisons, and slicers allow users to filter the data dynamically. Executive dashboards support data-driven decision-making by presenting information in a clear and visual format.

---

## 3. Execution Steps

1. Imported the dataset into Power BI Desktop.
2. Created measures for **Total Sales**, **Order Count**, **Total Orders**, and **Total Customers**.
3. Added four KPI cards to display the key business metrics.
4. Created a line chart to show the monthly sales trend.
5. Created a clustered bar chart to compare total sales by category.
6. Created a donut chart to display total sales by region.
7. Created a Top 10 Products by Sales chart using the Top N filter.
8. Added slicers for Year, Category, and Region to enable interactive filtering.
9. Added meaningful titles to all visuals.
10. Saved the dashboard and exported it as a screenshot and PDF.

---

## 4. Key Insights

- The dashboard provides a clear overview of overall sales performance.
- Sales performance varies across different product categories and regions.
- The Top 10 Products by Sales chart identifies the highest-performing products and supports better business decisions.

---

## Report Questions

### 1. What is the dashboard's main business message?

The dashboard provides a complete overview of business performance by displaying key sales metrics, monthly trends, category-wise sales, regional distribution, and top-selling products. It enables users to quickly understand sales performance and make informed business decisions.

### 2. Which filter changes the story most strongly?

The **Region** filter changes the story most strongly because it clearly shows how sales performance varies across different regions and highlights the best-performing areas.

### 3. What would you improve if you had one more day?

If I had one more day, I would improve the dashboard by adding more KPIs, applying a custom theme, improving the visual design, and adding drill-through pages for more detailed analysis.
